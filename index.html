Rules

How it works

  • 30 participants, 30 MLB teams, one team per participant each week. Teams rotate so everyone gets every team once.
  • Each new week starts on a Monday (week 1 runs from Opening Day to the first Sunday).
  • Every time a team scores exactly 13 runs in a game, the participants holding that team that week share the weekly prize.
  • If more than one team hits 13 in a week, the prize is split evenly per hit. A team that hits 13 twice earns two shares.

Payout

  • The weekly prize is $35, divided by the number of 13-run games that week.
  • If no team hits 13 in a week, the prize rolls over to the next week.
  • All winners are paid at the end of the year, at the steak dinner if you can make it. If you can't, we'll make separate arrangements.
  • Winnings shown here are not final until the end of the season, in case of a data error.

Communications

  • Check this page for weekly team assignments and winnings. Results update automatically every day.
